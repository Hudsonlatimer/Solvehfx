Hi Yuan,

Great chatting with you. Quick answers:

We're at 50 reports so far.

On talking to a user - I can't connect you with anyone, unfortunately.
Every report has been submitted anonymously, so I don't have contact info
for any of them. For the same reason I can't point you to specific feedback
like the pothole/car-damage example - I don't collect names or stories, just
the reports themselves.

On the tech - yes. When someone hits "submit," the report is emailed out
automatically within seconds. It goes to whichever authority is responsible
(HRM 311, Halifax Transit, or the province for highways) and to the
resident's district councillor. The emails come from a solvehfx.ca address
to the public government inboxes - it's not a formal partnership with the
city.

And no, reports don't show who submitted them. They're anonymous by
default; people can add an email if they want a reply, but that's optional.
